Ad Shield Privacy Policy

Ad Shield helps group administrators manage unwanted advertisements and spam.

Information processed

The bot may process Telegram user IDs, group IDs, message text, and links to detect unwanted promotions, depending on its actual features.

How information is used

Information may be used to moderate group messages and enforce group rules.

Data retention and contact

Data handling depends on the bot's actual implementation and hosting provider. Contact the bot administrator with privacy questions.
